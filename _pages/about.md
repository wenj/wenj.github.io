<div class="bio-block">
  <p>I’m a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at UIUC, advised by <a href="https://shenlong.web.illinois.edu/">Prof. Shenlong Wang</a> and <a href="https://www.alexander-schwing.de/">Prof. Alex Schwing</a>.</p>
  <p>My research focuses on digital avatars, with an emphasis on efficient, photorealistic, and animatable avatar reconstruction, as well as motion understanding.</p>
  <p>Before joining UIUC, I received my Master of Language Technologies from CMU and B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University.</p>
  <p>Check my latest Curriculum Vitae <a href="/files/CV_JingWen_Spring2026.pdf">here</a>.</p>
</div>
